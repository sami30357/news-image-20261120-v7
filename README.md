One image that sums up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026: 35 stories
across 7 regions, drawn as a classic cream broadsheet — but this time with one
dominant lead story. Kyiv's night attack fills the whole width as a layered
cityscape (skyline, river, three bridges, missile trails, fires), the cyclone
and the AI sell-off support it, and all ten drawn stories below are real
mini-scenes with sky and ground, not flat icons. The map's pins spread across
every continent, and the 22 briefs run in two balanced one-line columns.

Every story here happened, or was first reported, on Friday 20 November 2026.
Claims are attributed, not stated as fact. Mercury's orbit capture (Saturday)
and the GTA VI midnight launch (Thursday) are in the date check only — never
shown as Friday's news. The region bar's counts add up to 35.

Built for the [news-image-20261120 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120)
on make.fun. Referral code: 7b8zsyv7.

## How it runs

`render.py` (Python 3 + Pillow, no network) draws `entry.png` (1024×1024).
`main.ts` (Node 22) copies it to the output directory. The program runs twice
and writes the same bytes both times.
